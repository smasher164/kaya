# The android and linux lanes' wall, 2026-10-08

The full matrices of 2026-10-07/08 put android at 765-864 s net (870 ceiling)
and linux at 782-1121 s net (1250 ceiling). Every lane run below was taken in a
clean worktree at 81bb99b7 with the build caches cloned from the main tree,
since an uncommitted core change elsewhere did not compile for Android or GTK
at the time; linux and android ran together and no other lane ran.

## Where the time went (the matrices' own logs)

| | linux | android |
|---|---|---|
| token waits | 177-569 s | 122-455 s |
| core build | 3-6 s, or 216-267 s when kaya changed | 11-26 s (compose) |
| legs | 1187-1205 legs, 4627-4887 s of leg time on 8 sessions | 226-235 legs on 4 phones |
| serial | 7 exclusive legs, each emptying the pool; portfolio and varied alone | 7 ALONE legs, about 280 s one at a time (dnd 3 x 64 s, chat-go 50 s, tasks 30 s) |

Linux's swing from 855 to 1121 s net is its core build: kaya recompiled on
the matrices of 041444Z, 024715Z, 083831Z, 170924Z and the segmented one. A
greedy schedule of linux's legs at eight wide comes to 680-706 s, against a
legs phase of 1078-1128 s holding 245-305 s of token waits. The linux
container used 1.0-1.3 cores and at most 6.2 GiB of its 7.7 GiB during legs,
so its legs wait rather than compute and its width is bounded by memory.

## Not changed, for a ruling

Pooling the Android ALONE legs other than clock24-compose (the three drags,
chat-go, tasks-compose, notify-compose): run concurrently with each other they
passed 3 of 3 filtered runs (dnd 33-47 s, chat-go 47-48 s), and pooled with
the whole lane they passed in the one full run (lane 412 s against 598-715 s,
the run's one red the transport drop above on confirm-go). A greedy schedule
of the measured legs puts the saving at 190-210 s a lane. The set is the
maintainer's ruling of 2026-10-06/07 and the risk it guards shows only under
a full matrix, so it is left for that ruling and a matrix.

Widening the linux pool: legs are wait-bound, and a schedule at ten and twelve
wide comes to 573 and 522 s against 706 at eight. One run at KAYA_JOBS=10
passed in 573 s of legs with the container at 6.2 GiB; twelve would need the
Docker VM's memory raised above its 7.7 GiB first.

The token's waits, 122-569 s a lane, are now the largest single cost on both
lanes: on the segmented matrix android waited 230 s in its compose block for
the mac lane's filedialog, save and fullscreen legs.

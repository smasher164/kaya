THE SAME PROBE ON A PHONE-CLASS CORE: iPhone 17 Pro (A19 Pro), 2026-09-10
measured with tools/ios/gpuprobe/build.py, the app built, signed, installed
and launched on the maintainer's paired phone through devicectl; the report
is the app's own, copied back from its data container. The measurement
docs/measurements/canvas-gpu-timing-2026-09-10.txt said it was owed.

THE HOST
--------
iPhone 17 Pro, iOS 26.6.1, 6 hardware threads (vello_cpu's multithreaded
dispatcher takes 5 workers by its own rule), Level::new() = NEON, the
Apple A19 Pro GPU through Metal. The first launch aborted before any
measurement: wgpu's default limit table asks 16 inter-stage shader
variables and this adapter allows 15, so the device request failed —
both requests ask for the adapter's own limits now, and a refusal is
written into the report before the panic.

THE READING, AGAINST THE MAC
----------------------------
The CPU columns slowed by 1.3-2x (five slower workers instead of eight),
the GPU columns stayed where they were, and the crossover the mac record
predicted appeared in ONE place:

    scene, phone size 1200x2400      vello_cpu 5T   hybrid burst   classic burst
    portfolio chart                       0.30ms         0.58ms          0.59ms
    text page                             0.81           1.40            0.79
    2000 octagons                         1.13           1.55            0.56   <- the GPU wins, 2x
    10 big fills                          0.37           0.66            0.47
    50 big fills                          0.81           1.04            1.25
    200 big fills                         2.46           3.57            3.28
    800 big fills                         9.50          14.83           (blank frame: overflow)

Classic vello, the all-GPU renderer, beats the phone's five cores on the
one scene made of many small paths — 2,000 antialiased octagons, 0.56ms
pipelined against 1.13ms — which is what moving path flattening off the
CPU is for. It loses on every fill-heavy scene up to 200 full-screen
fills, and at 800 it drew nothing (its default bump buffers overflowed,
as on the mac). The hybrid loses everywhere, for the mac's reason: its
CPU half (10ms of strips at 800 fills) is the bottleneck.

THE BUDGET, WHICH IS WHAT THE RULING RESTS ON
---------------------------------------------
vello_cpu on five phone threads draws every scene inside the 16.7ms
frame: 800 full-screen translucent fills in 9.5ms (19.5ms on one
thread, so THREADS ARE NOT OPTIONAL ON THE PHONE for a heavy canvas —
the ledger's threads slice is the one that keeps this true), 2,000
octagons in 1.1ms, the chart in 0.3ms. So the standing ruling (no GPU
display path) holds on the phone as a matter of budget, and the
refinement is written down: an all-GPU renderer wins on path-count-heavy
frames on a phone, by about 2x at 2,000 paths, and a canvas that draws
tens of thousands of small paths per frame is where classic vello would
be measured again — with its overflow handling on, which its default
build lacks.

The single-frame `wait` on the phone is 1.27ms (1.52 on the mac), the
same fixed Metal round trip; readback at phone size adds 2.4ms to a
hybrid frame (4.00 against 1.56 on the chart), noticeably more than on
the mac's unified memory path.

# The media depth slice's mac measurements (2026-09-30)

Measured for docs/media-plan.md §6 on this tree's M5 Pro, macOS 26.6, with
throwaway probes that were deleted after (their sources and numbers were
kept in the session's notes). Every run that showed a window, took focus or
could make sound waited for the host to be idle 60 s or more (runs went at
85 to 306 s idle); players were muted or at volume 0.02; only the probe's own
window was photographed, by its window number.

## The video view's picture, by window capture

A bare `AVPlayerLayer` in an `NSViewRepresentable` in an `NSHostingView`,
paused at about 1 s, the clip 320x180 flat sRGB C83C1E.

| read | clip tagged bt709/bt709/bt709 | untagged | bt709 / sRGB transfer / bt709 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `screencapture -x -o -l<wid>`, raw (Display P3) | BF4E2F | BF4E2F | B9472C |
| the same converted to sRGB through CoreGraphics | CF4421 | CF4421 | C83C1E |
| in-process ScreenCaptureKit (desktopIndependentWindow), sRGB | CF4421 | CF4421 | C83C1E |
| `AVPlayerLayer.displayedReadOnlyPixelBuffer()` (macOS 26), paused | CF4421 | CF4421 | C83B1E |

ScreenCaptureKit from the probe itself, a child of the terminal holding
Screen Recording, raised no prompt, took 39 to 47 ms and gave the same bytes
as `screencapture`, stable across runs. The earlier probe's P3 BE4E2F
(docs/probes/video-native-2026-09-29/apple.md) is the same BT.709 transfer
shift. FFmpeg's `color` source builds its YUV with BT.601 whatever it is
tagged; built as RGB and converted with a BT.709 matrix, FFmpeg's own decode
reads C83B1E.

## Now Playing and the remote commands

A probe with the `.accessory` policy, playing muted or at volume 0.02,
publishing `nowPlayingInfo` and `playbackState` and registering
`MPRemoteCommandCenter` handlers. The system's view was read through
MediaRemote loaded inside Apple-signed `/usr/bin/perl`; loaded into the probe
itself it always answered pid 0 and no info.

| run | policy | audio | the probe is Now Playing | pause sent from perl | play sent from perl | playbackState after start / pause / play / stop |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | accessory | muted | yes | handler fired | fired | 1 / 2 / 1 / 3 |
| B2 | accessory | 0.02 | yes | fired | fired | 1 / 2 / 1 / 3 |
| B3 | regular | muted | yes | fired | fired | 1 / 2 / 1 / 3 |
| B4 | regular | 0.02 | yes | fired | NOT fired, sent and answered 1 | 1 / 2 / - / 3 |
| C2 | accessory | muted, playbackState never set | yes | fired | fired | 0 throughout |
| C3 | accessory | muted | yes | fired | fired | 1 / 2 / 1 / 3 |
| D | accessory | muted, sent from the probe's own process | yes | NOT fired, answered 1 | NOT fired | - |

Every command was sent only after the Now Playing pid, read just before,
was the probe's own. After the probe exits the pid returns to 0.

## Keep-awake

`preventsDisplaySleepDuringVideoPlayback = true` holds
`PreventUserIdleDisplaySleep` ("com.apple.coremedia.iq.ca.client-1") on the
process exactly while it plays, gone after a pause, absent with the flag
false (macOS's default). The media_session scene reads it by pid; it
releases about 1.9 s after a pause.

## The quiet case

A small AVFoundation probe (no window, no sound) over H.264 with MP2, Opus
and FLAC audio in MP4, MPEG-4 Part 2, H.264 4:4:4 and 10-bit, and VP9 in
MP4: every one plays except VP9 in MP4, whose video track is `isPlayable`
and `isDecodable` false with the asset `isPlayable` false and its AAC track
fine; the MP2 track is not listed at all. In the interpreter it reaches
ready and plays the audio with a 0x0 picture and no error unless the
decodability check fails it (the formats leg, with the check's flag cut:
"ready 2.0s 0x0, played past 1s, ended, can_play no").
